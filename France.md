# France

![Francepht](https://www.jmu.edu/global/abroad/_images/france_culture_banner.png)

## - Macarons
a delicate, colorful French sandwich cookie made from meringue and almond flour, typically filled with rich ganache, fruit curd, or buttercream, and known for its crisp exterior and chewy center

## - Éclair
a long, light French pastry made from choux dough, generously filled with a rich pastry cream or custard, and traditionally topped with a glossy chocolate glaze.

## - Crème Brûlée
a classic French dessert featuring a rich, chilled vanilla custard beneath a brittle, crackly crust of caramelized sugar
